Oregon's audit mix: performance audits fell four-fold; financial held flat

Across the five complete years 2020–2025, financial audits held roughly flat while performance audits — the type that asks whether a program achieved its purpose, and the type that produces findings and recommendations — fell from 16 to 4 per year. A financial audit attests that the books balance; the mix shifted toward attestation.
